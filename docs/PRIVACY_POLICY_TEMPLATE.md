**What stays on the device.** The picture a child is working on is saved only on that device

**Stickers and voice.** Which lessons were finished is kept on the device only. Lesson instructions can be read aloud by the device's own text-to-speech voice; no recordings are made and nothing the child draws is read or sent.

**Saving pictures.** If a grown-up chooses "Save picture", the picture is saved to the device
(web: downloaded by the browser). It is not uploaded by the app.

**Children.** The app is designed for young children. Because we collect no personal
information from anyone, there is nothing to share or sell. A simple math question protects the
grown-ups menu.
